YOUR SESSION

01 / CHOOSE A LESSON

Turn SELECT to browse. HOME returns to lessons. SCL opens scales; ARP opens arpeggios.

02 / PLAY & LEARN

PLAY starts a lesson. Follow the LCD and lit keys. Choose six difficulty levels. Practice chords, hands separately, timing, articulation, and dynamics.

03 / MAKE IT YOURS

GLO opens player, difficulty, game, and practice settings. PRESETS selects a DX7 sound. Four right knobs control the current screen.

04 / PRACTICE YOUR WAY

REC captures your playing. SEQ opens a 16–64 step polyphonic composer. SAVE keeps settings and progress in this browser.

SET THE CHALLENGE

Practice setup

One press of Start coaching begins a varied course. Each completed lesson moves on automatically after a short explanation and count-in. Repeating a lesson requires the explicit repeat option. Wait mode scores pitch, articulation and optional dynamics; onset timing is ungraded. A chord advances after every target tone is played. Adaptive repeats raise or lower tempo by 5 BPM from your score. Finger suggestions are visual cues; MIDI cannot detect which fingers you use.
REAL INSTRUMENT / MIDI SYSEX

Load a patch onto your FM-1

Current sound:

No patch has been sent. Select the actual device ports. Sending uses the selected sound and its DX7 parameter edits; browser reverb, delay, chorus and analog-engine sounds are not transferred.

Stock V16: the patch goes to the edit buffer; open OSC → SAVE → Preset on the device to store it. JIANT/Felucca: an FM6 patch acknowledgement is required; save on the device afterward. Unknown firmware is not targeted. Bank files can be exported from Sounds for use with your firmware’s librarian. These paths have protocol tests; physical-device verification is still required.
