VirtuoX source & credits

VirtuoX is an independent educational browser instrument by Richard Haberkern / telespial. It is not an installable teaching firmware image and does not flash or modify hardware. Physical firmware, timing, LEDs, storage and audio performance require a native port and hardware testing.

Public and personal sounds

This public edition includes the 128 M-VAVE FM-1 V15 factory voices, preserving names and order. The original factory source and decoded VMEM are included unchanged and verified against SHA-256 ecad0b8620f9d784d46c8da1e3c5de02c40214649ed8dec218026431046aad25. The strict browser synthesis engine requires 12 parameter adjustments, recorded in the repository’s compatibility-adjustments.json. The downloadable browser bank files contain these adapted voices. The source bank and its Apache-2.0 attribution are provided by the official m-vave/fm1_open repository.

Personal Yamaha ROM/Dexed banks are excluded. You can import compatible SysEx locally. Imported sounds and browser effects are not a firmware image. Analog-inspired sounds cannot be exported as DX7 voices.

Source and current behavior

Repository and complete browser source · Detailed manual · Firmware status and recovery.

The browser includes 72 studies, GLO subject mixing, complete-chord scoring, Player mode, patch editing, effects, an arpeggiator and a step sequencer. The sequencer scheduler tests actual note-event timing, including repeated notes with swing and cancellation of note-offs on Stop. Protocol tests do not replace physical FM-1 testing.
